# BoatAutonomy Profile Draft

BoatAutonomy is a governed marine-autonomy lab focused on staged
docking-assist research. The work combines marine telemetry, replayable edge
systems, safety boundaries, and multi-agent software engineering.

The public surface emphasizes what can be shown responsibly: passive telemetry,
synthetic demos, architecture, evidence discipline, and the engineering method
used to coordinate human and AI contributors. The private system remains
separate until specific artifacts are approved for release.

For professional context, see the public resume in `RESUME.md` and the
opportunity framing in `docs/relevant-work.md`.
